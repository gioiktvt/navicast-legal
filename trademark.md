## Title compliance check (Play Trademark Policy)

Per Google Play Trademark Policy, the **Title** of the listing **MUST NOT** contain trademarks of other companies. Acceptable titles:

✅ "NaviCast"
✅ "NaviCast — Watch Navigation Forwarder"
✅ "NaviCast — Forward Navigation to Smartwatch"
✅ "NaviCast — Chuyển hướng dẫn lên đồng hồ"

❌ FORBIDDEN (will be rejected):
- "NaviCast for Huawei Watch"
- "Huawei Maps Navigator"
- "Google Maps to Huawei Health"
- "Waze on Huawei"
- "Vietmap Watch"

The body description **may** mention trademarks, but only:
- In factual context ("works with Google Maps", "sends maneuvers to Huawei Watch GT5 Pro").
- With trademark notice (see disclaimer above).
- Without implying endorsement ("the official Huawei companion app" → ❌).

## Screenshot compliance

Per security policy + Play visual content policy:

| Item | Allowed? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Show app's own UI (Settings, Onboarding, Home) | ✅ | Native UI, no notification content |
| Show **mock** Google Maps navigation (e.g. demo route Hồ Gươm → Lăng Bác) | ✅ | Public landmarks; no real user nav |
| Show **real** Maps navigation with personal address | ❌ | Privacy violation — real address visible |
| Show GT5 Pro watch face with maneuver icon (mock) | ✅ | Watch face is generic Huawei UI |
| Show NaviCast logo | ✅ | Self |
| Show "Google Maps" logo or icon | ❌ | Trademark — except in screenshot of its own notification on phone (incidental fair use) |
| Show "Huawei Health" logo | ❌ | Trademark — except in onboarding screen 3 instructions where we say "open Huawei Health" with text, not logo |
| Show watch model name in screenshot caption | ⚠️ | OK as factual description ("Tested on Watch GT5 Pro"); avoid implying endorsement |

If screenshot includes a real Maps notification with addresses, **blur** before submission.

## Marketing copy guardrails (Play Console "Short description" + "Full description")

### Allowed phrases
- "Forward navigation icons from Google Maps to Huawei smartwatches"
- "Compatible with Huawei Band 2/3/4/6/7, Watch GT/GT2/GT3/4/5/6, Fit 2/3/4, Watch 3/4/5"
- "Works with Vietmap and Waze (Phase 2)"
- "On-device processing — no internet, no analytics"
- "Independent open beta — not affiliated with Google or Huawei"

### FORBIDDEN phrases
- "Official Huawei extension"
- "Endorsed by Google"
- "Powered by Google Maps API"
- "Huawei recommends"
- "Best Maps companion for Huawei"
- "Replaces Petal Maps" (this would be product comparison + potential trademark dispute)

### Recommended structure for "Full description"
1. Short pitch (1 sentence)
2. Feature list (3-5 bullets)
3. Compatible devices (factual list)
4. Privacy stance (1 paragraph: "no internet, no tracking, on-device only")
5. Quick setup (3 steps: install, grant notification access, enable in Huawei Health)
6. Trademark disclaimer (paste block above)
